About Sahaara

Sahaara is a cutting-edge AI-driven welfare monitoring platform specifically designed for uniformed forces. It addresses the unique mental health and stress challenges faced by military and emergency personnel.

Our mission is to provide comprehensive, proactive care that keeps personnel mentally and physically resilient, ultimately enhancing organizational effectiveness and personnel wellbeing.
