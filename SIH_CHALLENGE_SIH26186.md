## Problem Statement

### Background
Personnel serving in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), Armed Forces, and other uniformed services operate under:
- Physically demanding conditions
- Psychologically stressful environments
- Hazardous operational scenarios
- Extended deployments
- Separation from families
- Irregular working hours
- Exposure to traumatic incidents

**Current Challenge**: Stress identification depends largely on manual observation and self-reporting, which delays timely intervention.

### Required Solution
Develop an **AI-powered Personnel Stress and Welfare Monitoring System** that:
- Identifies early indicators of stress, burnout, and psychological distress
- Maintains privacy and organizational trust
- Enables proactive interventions
- Supports evidence-based welfare management
